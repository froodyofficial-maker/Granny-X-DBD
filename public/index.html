Survivors

You wake up in Granny's House (or her old one out in the woods: its front door is open, and a generator waits inside). Repair generators to power the exit gates, open a gate and escape before she finds you.

  • WASD move · Shift run · Ctrl/C crouch. Running is loud: she hears it.
  • Hold E: repair generators, heal, unhook survivors, open the gates
  • Space: vault, drop barricades on her, hit skill checks. A failed skill check is heard anywhere.
  • RMB flashlight: blind her for a moment
  • When carried, alternate A / D to struggle free
  • Two hits put you down. Hang on a hook too long, or get hooked a second time, and the Entity comes for you: hit its skill checks. A third hook is fatal. Space on your first hook to try to unhook yourself (3 tries, 4% each).

Granny

  • LMB swing the bat · hold E: pick up, hook, damage generators, break barricades
  • Hold RMB Bat Rush: wind up, release to charge. The longer you hold, the farther she goes.
  • R I Hear You: everyone running, repairing, healing or unhooking is revealed
  • Hold G Creep: vanish and reappear at a window, pallet, generator or hook
  • V Body Swap: switch places with Spider Mom (hold to look through her eyes first)
  • F Bedtime Trap execution · Q drop a survivor · Space vault

Grandpa

  • Hold RMB Shotgun: raise it, release to fire. Survivors have a second health bar against him: two shots put anyone down from mid range on, three point-blank, and every hit staggers.
  • R Bear Trap: set a trap where you stand (up to 3). Whoever steps in is injured, caught and revealed.

Slendrina

  • Hold RMB Haunt: aim at a spot up to 16m away (through walls too), release to vanish and reappear there.
  • R Cursed Gaze: for 8s, survivors who look at her are Cursed, slowed and revealed; look too long and her next hit downs you.
  • Survivors: when her eyes open, look away.

Angeline

  • Hold RMB Yellow Eyes: a beam from her eyes (16m). Half a second in it and a survivor is Spotted: her next hit downs them.
  • R Lullaby: survivors within 30m turn drowsy for 5s: they can't run.

Online

Host from Online Match (Public lobbies appear in everyone's lobby list) and send your friends the 5-letter code. They join from Online Match too, from anywhere. Bots fill empty slots and take over for anyone who disconnects.

Levels, sidekicks and seasons

Every character levels up on their own: each level gives a stat point to spend in Loadout. Killers can bring The Child of Slendrina (Store > Sidekicks): it crawls after you and shrieks when it sees a survivor nearby. Progress shows your ranks and the Season Pass. Two Killers is 2 vs 8.
